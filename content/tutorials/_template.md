## The decision

Describe the moment the reader faces this choice. Introduce one concrete example at the chosen depth.

## Try it on your project

Walk through the options and their trade-offs. Give the reader an action to take and a decision they can record. Include a prompt line or a runnable code example where it helps.

## Prove it

Give the reader a check they can perform and say what they should observe if it worked. Link to original sources beside the claims they support.
