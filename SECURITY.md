# Reporting a vulnerability

Use GitHub’s private reporting form to contact the VibeAcademy maintainers. Sign in, select **Report a vulnerability** on the repository’s Security tab and submit your report. [Open a private report](https://github.com/Shakilkhan24/VibeAcademy/security/advisories/new)

Include the affected page or feature, the version or commit if known, what you observed and who could be affected. Use synthetic examples and remove credentials, personal information and private prompts from attachments.

Keep the details in the private report while the maintainers investigate. They can ask follow-up questions and coordinate a fix with you there.
